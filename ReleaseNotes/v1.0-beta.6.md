## Leios updates itself now

Leios checks for new versions on its own and tells you when one is ready. It never installs
anything without being asked — you get a window describing what changed, and it updates when you
click **Install Update**.

**General → Updates** has the controls:

- **Check for updates automatically** — on by default. Turn it off and Leios only looks when you
  ask it to.
- **Include pre-release versions** — off by default. Turn it on to be offered betas like this one
  as they come out.
- **Check Now**, for when you don't want to wait.

There is also **Check for Updates…** in the Leios menu.

Both switches stay on the Mac you set them on, like the kill switches — iCloud does not carry
them. So you can follow the betas on one Mac and stay on stable releases on another.

### One manual step, this time only

Beta 5 and earlier have no updater, so they cannot offer you this release. Download it below and
drag it over your existing copy as usual. From here on Leios will do it for you.

Your settings, button mappings and Accessibility permission all carry across an update — nothing
to re-grant and nothing to set up again.

### Under the hood

Updates are signed, and Leios checks the signature before installing anything. The engine that
does the actual work runs as a separate background process living inside the app, so an update
restarts it onto the new version rather than leaving the old one running — which would otherwise
be invisible, with a new app driving an old engine.

## Scrolling works the way it was always supposed to

If you are on an Apple silicon Mac, this is the change you will feel first, and it is not a
tuning tweak — acceleration has never actually run.

Leios measures the gap between scroll ticks to decide how fast you are scrolling. It was reading
the clock on those events as though it were one kind of timestamp when macOS gives it another, and
on Apple silicon the two differ by a factor of about 42. Every gap therefore looked enormous, so
every tick looked like the start of a fresh, slow scroll: acceleration sat at its floor and fast
scroll never engaged, no matter how quickly you spun the wheel. Intel Macs were unaffected, which
is why it survived this long.

Scrolling a long page now takes the flick it should. Nothing in your settings changed — the
curves you already had are simply being applied now.

## A Statistics screen

A new row in the sidebar, showing what your mouse has actually done: **distance scrolled** in
metres, **scroll ticks** and flicks, **clicks** by button, **drag gestures**, and the **actions**
Leios fired for you. Over a week, a month, a year, or the lifetime of the install.

Distance comes in two figures where it makes sense — what the wheel asked for, and what Leios
delivered after acceleration. There is a time-of-day chart for the last week or month, so you can
see when the mouse is actually in your hand.

Counting is done by the engine in integer additions and written out once a minute from a
background thread. It is designed to cost nothing on the path your input travels, and it does not
touch scrolling latency.

**It is on by default and it never leaves your Mac unless you ask.** The switch is in
**Settings → Statistics**, along with **Reset Statistics…**, and it stays on this Mac the way the
kill switches do — turning statistics off on one Mac does not turn them off everywhere. If you use
iCloud sync, **Settings → iCloud → Include usage statistics** adds your other Macs' counts into
the same charts; turn it off and this Mac's counts are withdrawn from iCloud.

Counting only happens while Leios is running, so the numbers start from this update rather than
from the day you installed it.

## Nothing to redo

Your settings, per-app profiles, button mappings and Accessibility permission all carry across an
update. Leios can install this one itself if you are on beta 7 with **Include pre-release
versions** turned on.

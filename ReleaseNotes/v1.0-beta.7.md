## The settings window is built around a sidebar

Everything that used to be a tab is a row down the left now: **Scrolling**, **Apps**, **Buttons**,
**Devices**, **Settings**.

**Apps** is the one that really changes. It expands, and every app you have given its own scroll
settings sits underneath it as its own row — so a profile is one click away from anywhere in the
window, rather than a list tucked inside a tab with the settings squeezed beside it. Click an app
to edit it. To remove one: right-click its row, select it and press Delete, or use the button in
the title bar. Removing asks you to confirm now, which it never used to.

**Enable** has moved out of the toolbar to the top of the sidebar, and it says what the engine is
actually doing underneath it — **Running**, or what is wrong when it isn't. That was previously a
tooltip on a small dot, which is a poor place for the one thing you might need to read.

Two rows are renamed. **Info** is **Devices**, which is what it lists. **General** is **Settings**.

### The title bar

In dark mode the title bar stopped short of the window's left and right edges, and on the Apps tab
it lit only the half of the window the pointer happened to be over. Both are fixed, and the fix is
the sidebar: macOS draws that bar from the scrolling area beneath it, so a window split into two
scrolling areas side by side gets half a title bar. A sidebar is not one of those.

There is also a little less furniture. The window's title says which section you are looking at,
and an app profile puts its icon, name and bundle identifier up there instead of in a second strip
underneath.

### Nothing to redo

Your settings, per-app profiles, button mappings and Accessibility permission all carry across an
update. Leios can install this one itself if you are on beta 6 with **Include pre-release
versions** turned on.

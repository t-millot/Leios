## The settings window no longer comes up broken on a new Mac

Install Leios somewhere for the first time and the window could open wrecked: an empty sidebar
with no rows in it at all, and the settings pane clipped to a thin strip of whatever screen it was
on. Nothing to click, nothing to read, and no hint as to why.

It happened while the helper was waiting for Accessibility permission — which is to say, on every
Mac where Leios had just been installed, at the exact moment you first needed to be told what to
do. Granting the permission put the window right again instantly, so the one instruction that
would have fixed it was the one thing the window was too broken to show you.

The cause was the notice asking for that permission. Its text was set to size itself to its own
content, which sounds harmless and is how you would normally stop a paragraph being cut off. But
macOS measures that by asking how wide the sentence would be on a single line, and then treats the
answer as the narrowest the window is allowed to be. The window cannot be that wide, so the layout
was worked out for a window far larger than the one on screen, and everything landed outside it.
The paragraph wraps perfectly well on its own; the instruction was never needed.

The notice now reads the way it always should have, at every window size.

## Nothing to redo

Your settings, per-app profiles, button mappings and Accessibility permission all carry across an
update. Leios can install this one itself if you are on beta 9 with **Include pre-release
versions** turned on.
